# Brand — OMA3

Org-wide brand standards. Applies to every OMA3 property: the website, decks,
documentation, and collateral.

- [`brand-guidelines.md`](brand-guidelines.md) — logo, colour, typography,
  layout, components, imagery, and voice. The source of truth.

## Scope

This directory governs **how OMA3 looks and sounds**. It does not govern what
any individual page or deck *says* — that is written per-artifact. Website copy
lives in [`oma3dao/oma3-landing`](https://github.com/oma3dao/oma3-landing).

## Status

v1, written September 2026. It is the first written record of a brand that
previously existed only in a Webflow project and one investor deck. Where those
two disagreed, the guidelines record the conflict and which one won.

Two things follow from that:

- **Sections are labelled Observed, Decision, or Recommendation.** Recommendations
  are proposals for areas nothing currently defines. They are open to rejection
  and should be resolved rather than left standing.
- **The live site does not yet match this document.** The display typeface and
  the positioning statement are both changing. `oma3-landing` is the repo where
  the site catches up.

## Changing it

Open a PR. Brand decisions are worth a diff and a reason — that is the point of
keeping this in Git rather than in a document that gets edited in place.

When adding a value, label it. An unlabelled value is indistinguishable from
something somebody eyeballed, which is the failure mode
[§3](brand-guidelines.md#3-colour) exists to prevent.

## Assets

Brand assets are not yet in this repo. The list of what needs commissioning is
in [§11](brand-guidelines.md#11-assets-to-commission); an SVG wordmark is the
blocker. Once they exist they belong in `brand/assets/`.
